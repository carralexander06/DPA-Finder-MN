Before you sign up
  • Home Stretch© (in-person or live virtual through HOC partner agencies) is the main certified class that many MN down payment programs and first mortgages accept.
  • First-Generation Homebuyers Community DPA Fund accepts Home Stretch or Realizing the American Dream (instructor-led, in-person or live virtual). It does not accept Framework, Fannie Mae HomeView, or Freddie Mac CreditSmart.
  • Take the class before you sign a purchase agreement. Many programs require this (NeighborWorks Home Partners rule effective Oct 5, 2026; the First-Gen Community Fund also requires it).
  • The cost is often about $40. Some sessions are paid for by a sponsor, and fee waivers may be available, so call the agency.
  • Self-guided online Framework exists, but confirm with your lender and your DPA program before you rely on it.
