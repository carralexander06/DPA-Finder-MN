# Minnesota Down Payment Assistance Finder

A small educational page for looking up publicly described Minnesota down payment assistance programs.

Answer a few questions about where you want to buy, your household, and whether you are a first-time buyer. The page lists programs that may fit and links to each program’s own site so you can confirm the current rules.

This is not a loan application, a lender, or an offer of credit. It is not affiliated with any bank, lender, or government agency. Program names, amounts, income limits, and funding change often. Confirm eligibility and terms with the program administrator or a licensed housing counselor before you rely on them.
